# No tensions

The rebuild has no concept of tensions. The old game tracked records like Stalked, WoundedPrey and FoodScentStrong to raise the odds of scripted events; they had to be kept in sync with the world and could claim a wolf that did not exist. What they stood for is now just the world: a pack that is actually following you, a wounded animal that split from its herd, meat in your pack. Nothing summarises it into a separate value.
