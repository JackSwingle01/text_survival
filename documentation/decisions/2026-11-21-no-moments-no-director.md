# No moments and no director

The rebuild has no event popups ("moments") and no director pacing what the player notices. Discoveries and ordinary gameplay are the moments: things on the map and in the action list that the player answers with normal actions. A moment system was designed (a real dynamic crossing a perceptible threshold, choices built from what is present) and rejected as a lot of machinery for little gain: most games have their moments without prompting the player.

**Considered options:** authored events (the old game: high authoring cost, tensions that could lie); moments plus a director that biased reveal timing (rejected here).
