# A body is read in tiers, and the world reads only its faculties

A body is read in tiers, each reading only the one below: its state and effects, then blood and
breath, then its capacities (moving, manipulation, consciousness, sight, hearing), then its four
faculties (strength, speed, dexterity, perception), then vitality. Everything outside a body reads
faculties and nothing else. A verb is refused when the faculty it needs is at zero, and slowed by
how far that faculty is below a fresh body of its kind. Zero means the part gives nothing: no
working leg is speed at zero, no working arm is dexterity at zero, and an unconscious body's limbs
give nothing, so every verb that needs a faculty is refused to it. Vitality is one overall read
taken from the four faculties, for whoever is looking at the body; nothing inside a body reads it.
Whether a body is alive is a separate read, from its heart, lungs and brain alone.

We turned down three things. Vitality inside every faculty's formula, as the rebuild had it: it was
the least of consciousness, blood pumping and breathing, so whichever was lowest reached every
faculty, and a man short of sleep lost most of his strength. Refusing on a capacity below a tuned
threshold: the number had no meaning, and it let verbs reach inside the body. A fifth faculty for
the mind: it would have passed consciousness through under a second name and added a line to every
verb, where an unconscious body's limbs giving nothing needs no new part.

It costs two things we accept. A body with no working arm cannot feed itself, because eating and
drinking need dexterity above zero. Strength stays above zero on an unconscious body, on purpose: a
body keeps what its size gives it.

The tiers are separate types, held by dependency rules in the test project: nothing outside the body
uses capacities, capacities do not depend on faculties, faculties do not depend on vitality, and
there are no cycles. The places that read a capacity from outside today are a list that only
shrinks; when it is empty, capacities are hidden from the rest of the sim. The screen has one named
read-out, because the UI shows what the game knows.
