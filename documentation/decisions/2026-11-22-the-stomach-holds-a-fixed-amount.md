# The stomach holds a fixed amount

The stomach holds about 1.5-2 kg, and eating is refused when it is full. Food digests over hours, and
its calories go to fat as it digests, so nothing eaten is wasted. This keeps the eat-now-or-carry
choice: you cannot turn a carcass into fat in one sitting.

**Considered Options:** the old game let you eat past full and wasted the excess, with a warning; the
rebuild turned all food into fat with no limit; a fixed stomach that refuses at full (chosen), which
needs no warning, since the refusal says why (decision "The UI shows what the game knows").
