# Transformations are recipes worked by a station, at a rate set by exposure

Cooking, melting snow, rendering fat, drying food and curing hides are recipes whose work is done by a station (a fire or a curing rack), not a person. One in progress is a transformation, and its rate comes from the exposure at the station: a fire's temperature for a recipe driven by heat, and warm air, wind and falling rain or snow for one driven by drying. A recipe's time is its time at full rate (a good fire; a dry, still day). This replaces the rebuild's cooking and melting by hand and the old rack's plain timers. It reuses the one recipe shape and the one exposure calculation, so a rack beside a fire going faster, or one under a shelter drying through a storm, needs no rule of its own.

A transformation makes a different thing and never goes back. A state that rises and falls on the same thing (spoiling, frozen, wet) is an effect, and stays one.

**Considered Options:** timed slots on the fire and rack (the old game: a second making system beside Make, blind to fire and weather); drying as an effect on the item (needs the effects kernel for things, and gives up the recipe's times); recipes worked by a station (chosen).

**Consequences:** a rack's "48 h" is an expected time, shown from the forecast. Cold alone does not slow drying. Ownership of what sits at a station is left to the NPC work; until then it belongs to nobody.
