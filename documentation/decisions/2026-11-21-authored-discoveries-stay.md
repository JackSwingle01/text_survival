# Authored discoveries stay until the world produces them

The discovery content already ported (named places, hidden finds, discovery events) keeps working and is retired only when the emergent system produces the same thing; authored finds may carry authored causes. "Every sign has a true cause" is a preference, not a rule. A cheaper settling pass that placed camps and bodies with invented causes ("ghost camps") was rejected for now; real NPC travellers will produce them once NPCs survive a season.
