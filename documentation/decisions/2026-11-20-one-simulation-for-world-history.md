# One simulation for world history

The valley's history (old kills, worn trails, camps) is produced by running the real sim forward at world generation, not by a second, cheaper model of herds and travellers. A separate coarse model was the converged design for the corridor game, but beside the rebuild's full per-minute agents it would drift from them, and what the player finds would not behave like the world they stand in. The cost is speed: distant agents have to step coarsely for a long settle to be affordable.
