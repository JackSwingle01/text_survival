# No special cases: responses emerge from general systems

What a person can do about a situation comes only from systems that exist for everyone: fighting, moving, waiting, fire, digging. Nothing scripts "what you can do when a bear is near". New behaviour arrives as small general mechanics (fire's heat and light is a term in predator boldness), never as per-situation choices, so every situation updates when a mechanic does. A reader will see no "drive off with fire" action and no guarded-kill handling; that is deliberate.
