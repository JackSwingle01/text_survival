# Animals are individuals; a herd is derived

Every animal is its own agent, with its own body, plan and mind. A herd is the animals of one species
standing on one tile, worked out when something needs it and never stored. Herd size comes from two
forces in the same currency the planner uses: shared vigilance, a benefit that levels off, against
sharing the forage, a cost that keeps growing. There are no split or merge rules. The alternative was
the herd as the agent, with rules to split it and merge it: one decision for many bodies, and
cohesion guaranteed. We turned it down because a tile is 100 m, so the only difference the player
can see is herds splitting and joining across tiles, and individuals give that without a rule of its
own. Measured on 2026-11-22, one agent per animal costs about 2-2.5x the animal side of a sim day and
is invisible in play. Packs are closed groups made by familiarity, not a stored pack id. This stands
only if the cohesion gate passes, which checks that open-group animals do not scatter, that herds do
not grow into one blob, and that packs hold. If it fails, the fallback is herd agents with split and
merge rules.
