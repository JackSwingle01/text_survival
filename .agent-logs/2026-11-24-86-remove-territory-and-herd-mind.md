# #86: Remove the fixed territory and the old herd mind

Hypothesis:
- Changes: Fauna (Fauna.cs, Needs.cs, Kin.cs, Scavenging.cs), Populate, Renewal, Agents.Spawn, Memory (dens), Clues (game near), Tags (Attending, Unaware), Web observer, Tools/Tune Fights, docs ("Agent behaviour" row, overview.md), save fixture.
- Affects: `HerdState` and its five decide functions (DecidePrey/Pack/Solitary/Scavenger/Opportunist), PatrolStep, Drift, the pack timeout walk home, Stalk on the patrol clock; `HerdMind.Territory`/`TerritoryIndex` read as den (`Territory[0]`: rest source, open-sleep factor, `Memory.Dens`), as land (Clues `GameNear`/`RangesNear`, renewal capacity per herd, arrival land off pack land), and as placement (Populate flood fills, `Escaped`). Vigilance (`PreyNotices`), sighting pools, `BeginHunt` awareness, `Tags.Unaware`/`Attending` read the state today.
- Design: `HerdMind.Den` (TileId?) replaces the territory; fleeing is `FleeFrom != null`; what an animal is doing is its current activity (`Sim.ActivityOf`). One `Decide`: flee, else planner (`Needs`), else the mind's group and role rules (come for people, cohere, perch/follow/raid for opportunists), else eat what is on its tile at Want, else rest. Renewal capacity is `ValleyCapacity` per species.
- Stays green: planner tests (IndividualsTests, PackTests, ForecastTests, ScavengerTests), cohesion gate seeds 17/23/42/123, SaveFixtureTests after regeneration, the fast suite.
- First red: a new architecture test (`Fauna_HasOneMind`) fails while `HerdState` and `DecidePrey` exist.
- Baseline: `report 60` on seeds 17, 23, 42, 123 from origin/main before the change (numbers below).

Baseline `report 60` (origin/main 1ffee3ab), day 60, seeds 17/23/42/123, summed: Wolf 15, Bear 13, CaveBear 15, SaberTooth 6, Hyena 33, Caribou 48, Megaloceros 23, Bison 120, Mammoth 12 (seed 123 only), Fox 12, Raven 49. About 150 s a seed.

Revised (review): `Sim.ActivityOf` is null for an animal outside its own turn (one-minute activities end within it), so vigilance, sighting pools and `Tags` cannot read the activity → `HerdMind.Choice` keeps a copy of what `Decide` last chose, and `FledMinute` stands for "just stopped running".
Revised (review): `ValleyCapacity` is 0 for species with no arrival row (Mammoth, CaveBear) and its rate was tuned for arrivals → `RenewalRow.CapacityPerScore` now means per unit of valley score, set from the median over seeds 1, 5, 17, 23, 42, 123 of each species' summed old territory capacity ÷ valley score (probe on origin/main); arrivals keep their own `ArrivalRow.ValleyPerScore` trigger and room.
Revised (review): putting ComeForPeople before Needs would send a thirsty wolf after a person → order is Needs, Cohere, ComeForPeople, Scavenge, Idle, with the old DecidePack thresholds (hunt here above 0.4 hunger awake or above 0.6; stalk above 0.6 on the 30-minute clock, 5 when starving).
Revised (review): `AnimalFoodPriority` never drops below Want, so "eat at Want" would graze around the clock → Idle keeps the old prey hysteresis (start grazing above 0.5 hunger, keep on above 0.2, read from `Choice`), and a meat-eater feeds on meat on its tile above 0.1 (the old pack's feed-then-rest).
Revised (review): stealth and `DetectionRangeTiles` switched on species names → content rows `stealth` and `detectionRangeTiles` in species.json; Fauna.cs is off the species-names ratchet.
Revised (review): hyenas fled a wolf/bear/saber-tooth at a kill by name → `HeldKill` covers the Scavenger role too: a predator of another species on the tile holds the kill.
Revised: Populate's random territory flood fill both drew rolls and spaced predators → `Reach` (breadth first, no roll) within `SpacingTiles` (6) checks a start is not penned in and is the ground taken off-limits to the next predator; placements reshuffle once, accepted.
Revised: Clues read "game near" as a herd whose land held the tile → any live animal within `Clues.RangesNearTiles` (10, about the old land's reach; bison land ran to 400 tiles).
Revised: `Escaped` gave the escaper a scrap of land → it only starts fleeing; what makes it run alone is unchanged (it is the one that got away, the herd stays).
Revised: FireDeterrenceTests' pack walked onto the player's tile because its land ran there and it patrolled → the pack dens on the player's tile and is tired out of its hours, so it makes for its den (rest plan) and is held off by the fire.
Revised: Pre-existing tests changed: HerdGoldenTests vigilance rows now set `Choice`/`FleeFrom`/`FledMinute` for the old state and minutes; PredatorEngagementTests' pack no longer sets a patrol state or clock (the tests' minutes fall on the 30-minute stalk clock); NetProgress reads "wants to move" as fleeing or a plan with a route ahead (patrolling is gone); RenewalTests read one capacity per species (`Fauna.Capacity`), arrivals by tile, wolf arrivals off pack dens by `SpacingTiles`; the ravens' thinning test puts 24 ravens over a valley carrying about 16.
Revised: Dropped `HerdTests.APatrollingPack_RangesAcrossItsLand` (patrols are gone; `PackTests` roaming test covers a pack ranging when hungry), `HerdTests.PreyLand_MayOverlapAPack` (no land to overlap; prey are placed anywhere outside the safe zone, pack ground included), `TerritorySweepTests.SmallestTerritory_PerSpecies_IsRealRange` (no territory; `DenTests.EveryAnimal_StartsOffThePass_WithGroundToWalk` checks every start is off the pass and not penned in). `DenTests.EveryTerritory_IsWalkableFromItsStart_AndOffThePass` became that test; `TheValley_IsPopulatedLikeTheOldGame` checks dens and pack spacing in place of land sizes.
Revised: an arrival's room was what a random new territory carried less who already lived on it, so most caribou arrivals (5 or more) found no room → room is now the valley's capacity less the species' count, so arrivals come more often into a valley short of a species; `RenewalTests.Prey_CalveInTheirSeason` now counts calves (new on a mother's tile at midnight) out of season, not head count, since herds still arrive then.
Revised: idle, an animal lay up all day → in its active hours it is up (Wait), out of them it lies up (Rest), as the old pack patrolled when active (PlannerTests' wolves at dusk and hyenas at night).
Revised: `PackTests.RivalPacks_GiveWay_AndKeepApart` relied on fed packs patrolling their land → the packs are hungry with nothing to hunt, so they roam on the planner and meet; `HerdTests.ALameAnimal_StaysBehind` relied on drift → the herd is thirsty and sets out for water.
