# NPCs wash (#266)

## Hypothesis

Today `Npc.Decide` never chooses `Wash`: `Stat` has Warmth, Water, Food, Rest, and `Keep()` only walks home,
stops a melt, tends the night fire, sleeps or rests. Dirty builds 0.004/h plus sweat and carcass work and never
fades, so every NPC ends at full Dirty.

Design:

- `Stat.Dirty` joins the enum and is last on `Priorities.Ladder`. `Priorities.DirtyPriority(severity)`: Want above
  0.5, else None; never Need. `StatPriorities` gains `Dirty` with a default of None, so the animals (`Needs.cs`)
  and the player's `Priorities.Of` are unchanged (neither reads it).
- `Npc.Mind.ForWash`: the `Wash` verb here when not refused; else a trip by `Planner.Choose` to a tile where the
  view forecasts washing water (`Work.WashingWaterAt`, the verb's own water rule read through a `WorldView`:
  open water, a pond, snow), filtered by `Survives` as water and food trips are. Interrupts are the plan's
  usual ones, so a Need or Critical breaks it.
- `Keep()`: where it would rest or sleep, an NPC with any Dirty washes first if `Wash` is not refused. No trip.
- Risks: food is never None (`FoodPriority` returns Want at least), so Dirty's Want is reached only when food's
  Want finds nothing to pursue; washing whenever dirty at all, before idling, wets the NPC (0.05 a wash) every
  idle stint. Both are the rule as given; the bench reports what they do.

Seams: `Npc.Decide` on a hand-built world (`ConservationWorlds.Bare`), and `NpcDriver.Live` for the trip.
