# #269: One fire forecast: can it be lit, how long and how hot it burns, and what is missing

Hypothesis:
- Changes: `Fire` (new `Fire.Forecast` with its ask and answer records; `UsableHours`, `WillTakeALog`, `LightForecast.KeepsGoingPct`/`Takes`/`CatchTemperatureF` deleted), `Npc` (compile-only: `UsableHoursAt` and `CanLight` read the forecast), `Priorities` (comment), `GLOSSARY.md` Forecast entry, the Forecast row in `documentation/clean-room-architecture.md`.
- Affects: the warmth priority's fire hours (now the forecast's stepped burn, not `HoursItWillBurn` + reckoned `FuelHours`); the NPC's `CanLight` (was "a log will catch a minute after the strike", becomes "the fuel on offer makes the fire burn longer than the strike's flame alone", so sticks now count); Firecraft pay on a struck fire (`PayLighter`, unchanged rule).
- Stays green: `PrioritiesTests` (it passes hours straight to `WarmthPriority`, so no case changes), `FireStartingTests`, `SkillsTests`, `NpcTests`, the fast suite.
- First red: `FireForecastTests.TheTwoStates_FromTheNpcDeaths` fails to compile on `Fire.Forecast`, then fails on 4.4 kg of sticks.

Design: the forecast steps a copy of the fire minute by minute, the strike first when the pit is cold, each minute putting on Next (the first fuel on offer, in order of catch temperature, that the fire will take and has room for, as much as fits). Hours, least heat and the fuels that never went on all come from that one burn. Missing is built by adding each entry to a copy of the offer and burning again: tinder when a strike is needed and none is on offer, then one bridge (sized also to reach the hours) when fuel on offer never went on, else one more-hours entry. Weights are found by doubling then halving against the burn.
