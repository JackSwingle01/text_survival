- Changes: `Trip` (new public `Trip.Routes` search returning a `RouteMap` of minutes, kcal and paths; `Trip.StepMinutes` loses its herd refusal and prices herds and flyers), `Traversal` (the herd crossing rule `HerdCrossingMinutes` goes; herds cross by `Trip.Terms` at their slowest member's body; flyers at a flat cost per tile; a flyer-aware block rule), `Npc` (its private Dijkstra replaced by `Trip.Routes`), `Fauna` (private BFS `FirstStep` and A* `Route` replaced by `Trip.Routes`), `species.json`/`SpeciesStats` (a flight field; Raven flies), `BodySurvival`/`Sim` (a public per-minute travel burn so the route's kcal is the tick's).
